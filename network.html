Railwire AGNP

A leading Area Gateway Network Partner for RailTel

Since 2020, PCNC has partnered with RailTel Corporation of India Ltd. as an Area Gateway Network Partner (AGNP) for Railwire in North Bengal. Today we are a leading Railwire AGNP in the region, actively managing a network of 138 Access Network Partners (ANPs) who extend last-mile connectivity to communities across North Bengal.

  • Trusted RailTel Railwire AGNP entity
  • 138 Access Network Partners under management
  • Nationwide-grade broadband, delivered regionally

Fiber infrastructure

Proprietary underground & overhead fiber

We are continuously developing and deploying proprietary underground and overhead fiber-optic networks across the entire North Bengal region. Owning our fiber assets means greater control over quality, resilience and uptime, and a foundation for the region's future bandwidth needs.

Beyond our own network, PCNC provides critical underground optical fiber network (OFC) support and route maintenance for RailTel Corporation of India, a testament to our engineering and field-operations capability.
