Company overview

A technology-driven leader in high-speed connectivity

PCNC PRIVATE LIMITED is a premier telecommunications and digital infrastructure provider operating across the strategically vital region of North Bengal. With operational roots extending back to 2015 and formal corporate incorporation under the MCA in early 2024, the company has established itself as a robust, technology-driven leader in high-speed connectivity solutions.

Specialising in Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) architectures, enterprise networking, optical fiber line maintenance and next-generation broadband delivery, PCNC drives regional digital transformation. Through its collaborative ecosystem, including its sister concern CCN Broadband, the company delivers seamless, high-availability internet and data services across residential, commercial and institutional segments as a trusted Railwire Area Gateway Network Partner (AGNP).

Meet our founder

Director & Founder PD

Pintu Dey

Director & Founder, PCNC PRIVATE LIMITED
B.Com, North Bengal University (2002) 19+ Years in Telecom Fiber & Cable Distribution

Pintu Dey brings more than 19 years of hands-on experience across telecom, cable distribution and fiber infrastructure. His journey began in 2007 as a local cable operator in Raiganj, a foundational last-mile access experience that shaped a distribution-first, customer-close philosophy.

Over the following years he became the official Tata Teleservices distributor for Raiganj, scaled to become a leading telecom super stockist in North Bengal for Bharti Airtel, and then pioneered ISP operations under CCN Broadband. Today he leads PCNC's expansion into the IP-1 domain and its role as a primary Railwire AGNP entity.
