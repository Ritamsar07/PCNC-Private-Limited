Company growth & milestones

Nearly two decades of building connectivity

The journey that led to PCNC PRIVATE LIMITED.

2007

Local Cable Operations, Raiganj

Began as a local cable operator (LCO) in Raiganj, gaining foundational last-mile access experience.

2009

Raiganj Distributor for Tata Teleservices

Appointed the official distributor for Tata Teleservices Pvt. Ltd. (Tata Indicom) in Raiganj.

2009 to 2014

Telecom Super Stockist, North Bengal

Scaled distribution as super stockist for Bharti Airtel, becoming a leading telecom distributor in North Bengal.

2015

Entry into Broadband Services

Pioneered local ISP operations in Raiganj under CCN Broadband.

2016

Bandwidth Distribution & Fiber Maintenance

Expanded regional bandwidth distribution with Meghbela Broadband and established last-mile fiber maintenance teams for WBSEDCL across Malda, Uttar Dinajpur and Dakshin Dinajpur.

2020

Joined RailTel as Railwire AGNP

Partnered with RailTel Corporation of India Ltd. as an Area Gateway Network Partner (AGNP) for Railwire in North Bengal.

2024

Incorporated PCNC PRIVATE LIMITED

Formally incorporated to expand into the IP-1 domain, becoming a primary Railwire AGNP entity and growing physical dark-fiber and underground network assets.

Today

21,000+ subscribers & 138 ANPs

A leading RailTel AGNP in North Bengal, actively managing 138 Access Network Partners across six districts, with proprietary fiber deployment underway.
